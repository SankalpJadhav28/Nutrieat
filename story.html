Who's Behind Nutrieat

Built by friends in our 20s who wanted better everyday food.

Right outside Sahyadri Ground in Tilak Nagar, we saw everyone finishing morning runs, cricket matches, and gym workouts with nowhere affordable to grab clean, high-protein fuel.

So we started Nutrieat—bringing real rolled oats, whey protein smoothies, creamy peanut butter, and toasted sandwiches straight to the neighborhood at honest prices.
