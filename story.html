Our Startup Story

FROM A MORNING IDEA TO SAHYADRI GROUND.

No big investors or fancy cloud kitchens—just friends in our 20s who wanted clean, high-protein food to be affordable and delicious every single morning.

01
Chapter 01 • The Spark

The Post-Workout Dilemma

Every morning after running and hitting the gym around Tilak Nagar, we faced the exact same problem: either grab oily street snacks or spend ₹350+ at a trendy cafe for a simple bowl of oats.

"Why isn't clean protein fuel ₹80 instead of ₹350?"

That single question after a morning session at Sahyadri Ground kicked off everything.

02
Macro Precision
Weighed & Tested

Every gram of oats, peanut butter, honey, and whey measured so you know your exact macros.

Chapter 02 • Kitchen Trials

Dialing In Taste & Macros

We tested dozens of oat-to-milk ratios, roasted peanut butter drizzles, and multigrain sandwich combos on our friends until healthy food actually tasted like a treat.

03
Chapter 03 • Going Live

Setting Up Opposite Building No. 2

With our custom standee, fresh morning prep, and zero-pretension street energy, we opened our counter right outside Sahyadri Ground—serving walkers, cricketers, and gym regulars from 7 AM to 11 AM.

Morning Rush @ Sahyadri Ground

04
Evolving With You

When regulars asked for a non-chocolate bowl and post-workout shakes, we added Classic Honey & Nut Oats and Whey Protein Smoothies straight to the lineup.

Chapter 04 • What's Next

Making Everyday Health A Habit

We're just getting started. From stall fitness challenges to new high-protein drops, our goal is simple: make Nutrieat Mumbai's favorite morning ritual.
